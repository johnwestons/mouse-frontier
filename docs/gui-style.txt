ACTION MENU SPRITE STYLE

The compact sprite atlas in assets/sprites/ui/actions/simple is used by the
radial, box and dock action-menu layouts. It keeps those quick-access controls
easy to scan while the title screen, HUD, inventory, settings, crafting,
minigames and other established game screens keep their authored GUI styling.

Advanced GUI editing continues to support action-menu control placement and
scale. The title-screen control spacing remains separated for reliable access.
