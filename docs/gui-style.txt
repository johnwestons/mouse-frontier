SHARED SIMPLE GUI STYLE

The default GUI uses the approved dark brown sprites with thin amber outlines
and cream labels. game/gui_art.lua shares the rectangular and circular frames
from assets/sprites/ui/actions/simple across the action layouts, title screens,
HUD, inventory, dialogue, shops, settings, editor, radio, minigames and co-op UI.

Nine-slicing keeps borders narrow at different panel sizes. Texture coordinates
also scale for packaged images. Selected and unavailable controls retain state
feedback; high-contrast settings retain their additional outlines. Existing
advanced-editor color, position, scale and alternate-frame overrides still work.
Characters and item illustrations remain their authored sprites.

The title screen's Cheats / Controls and Local Play buttons occupy separate
ends of the footer, with fitted text inside their touch targets.

- Targeted UI, editor, touch, inventory, repair and crafting behavior tests.
- Companion activity, checkpoint, title and Local Play behavior tests.
