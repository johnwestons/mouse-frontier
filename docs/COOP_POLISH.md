# Co-op client polish

Active goal requested October 20, 2026.

The user's physical test connected successfully with a Windows PC hosting and an Android phone joining. Client movement was the worst source of lag, followed by scene transitions. The stop's sword-swing action and gun range were missing or inaccessible. Clients must also be able to enter and leave local scenes, such as homes, without moving the host. Train travel and other story advancement remain host-controlled.

The user expanded this goal: both host and client must choose an existing save slot inside Local Play and launch from that menu. The client brings that slot's own traveler, inventory, ammunition, weapons, XP and upgrades. Co-op gains and gifts must return to that same slot on the client's device and remain usable in solo play or another friend's world. Direct giving through inventories must include ammunition reserves; using a shared chest is insufficient.

## Work and acceptance

1. **Movement and transitions:** measure client prediction, state reception, asset preparation, and frame stalls. Reduce avoidable work and correction stutter without accepting client positions or letting stale inputs move indefinitely. Check desktop and phone-like frame rates, jitter, loss, reconnects, and scene loading.
2. **Ordinary actions:** make the client's stop attack available through keyboard, controller, and touch. Keep owned equipment, damage, cooldowns, and rewards authoritative. Audit nearby interactions and menus for further host/client gaps.
3. **Independent local scenes:** track each player's scene and local doorway separately. Entering or leaving a home must preserve the other player's position and controls. Collision, visible actors, interaction reach, inventory, NPC conversations, saves, and reconnects must use the acting player's scene. Host-controlled travel safely reunites the party. Do not grant clients story progression through a local scene command.
4. **Concurrent activities:** make the gun range discoverable and usable by either player, including both practicing simultaneously. Keep aim, controls, ammunition, score, activity state, and completion receipts separate. One player's menu or practice must not pause the other's simulation. Extend the same ownership rules to the existing maintenance, repair, sewing, and first-aid activities where applicable.
5. **Direct sharing:** let either player give selected backpack/equipped items and a chosen quantity of reserve ammunition to the other nearby player. Preserve gear condition and identity, full-pack failures, durable receipt retries, and ownership.
6. **Portable travelers:** select the local slot in Local Play on both devices. Keep world/story progress in its own save, transfer only the client's personal character, and save acquired/lost items and progression back to the selected local slot. A durable visit handoff must prevent reconnect, crash, or repeated return receipts from duplicating gear or XP.
7. **Verification:** use regression tests and native two-peer runs, report timing and remaining gaps, and distinguish simulated phone controls from physical Android testing. Rebuilt packages must match before another PC/phone test.

## Initial findings

- Ordinary movement already has client prediction and a separate motion channel. It uses 20 Hz input, 12 Hz snapshots, and a 350 ms stale-input cutoff. Profile these paths before changing rates.
- Reliable state refresh currently decodes and validates the full assembled world, then the guest makes another complete presentation copy and prepares scene/character assets. These are candidates for transition and periodic frame stalls; their cost is not yet measured.
- Client field attacks are explicitly restricted to expedition scenes, explaining the missing stop action.
- House entry and exit currently move the entire party and are classified as host progression. Independent local scenes require actor-specific location and authorization, not simply exposing the host's buttons.
- Gun-range sessions are already keyed by player. Audit access, presentation, network waiting, and concurrency before replacing this existing implementation.

No new character dialogue is authorized by this goal.
