# Home-stop introduction

New journeys begin on foot at the old train yard at stop 1. One continuous track curves from the background onto the parked train's rail and continues off the foreground edge, following the user's red route. The station office is a separate transparent exterior sprite with its own enterable interior. The tortoise conductor stays inside the office while the apprentice prepares to travel west.

Approach the office door and enter, then speak with the conductor to read the five approved opening lines. Return outside, load the supply crates, service the engine at the repair stand, then return inside for the approved farewell. Leave the office and approach the boarding marker to enter the train. Travel starts the regular westbound journey at stop 2. The conductor and mechanic remain behind.

The adult tinker fox follows the user's green route: supplies, repair shed, near engine fittings, around the locomotive to the far platform, then back around the front to the supplies. It pauses at each of the four marked work stops: lifting a supply crate, working with a wrench at the shed and near engine, and oiling the far fittings. The October 19 correction replaces the younger Picture Shop worker strips with a dedicated adult set under `assets/sprites/home-stop/adult-tinker-fox`. Eight repaired authored walk views match the existing adult directional idles and carry action. Wrench and west-facing oiling loops preserve their original adult artwork. All 19 strips use uniform 512px cells, one 82px world body canvas, and a 458px foot baseline; deliberate crouching and arm/tool lean remain in the artwork. `game/home_stop_fox.lua` runs the patrol with distance-driven gait, fixed work positions, and persisted route/animation progress in `npcStates`. Old checklist work states migrate to the matching adult action without losing route progress.

Approach the fox and use Talk by keyboard, mouse, or touch. Talk uses only the already approved `Catalog.dialogueLines`, unchanged. The fox faces the player in the matching adult idle pose, pauses while talking, and resumes the same route/work phase when dismissed. Talk does not advance the conductor's story or grant preparation rewards. Chatter and its relationship record use the existing host-authoritative NPC chatter command, including a live reach check; the guest's home-stop people panel exposes Talk only. No new character speech was written.

The introduction teaches movement, nearby interactions, the backpack, the four supply types, train condition, and departure costs. Instruction text follows keyboard remapping and touch controls. The first service is free; later services use the existing onboard Maintenance activity. The yard has no random combat, residents, houses, or optional quest interruptions.

On wide screens, the introduction title, current objective, and controls occupy the empty right sidebar beneath the scene buttons. The panel measures its wrapped text at the actual sidebar width, including standard 16:9 windows. Office entry/exit, conductor and fox talk, supplies, repair, and boarding prompts use a separate card beneath the instructions. Character names and action text no longer float over the home-stop scene. Nearby prompts show the bound interaction key, gamepad button, or touch action; distant prompts ask the player to approach. A compact right-edge layout handles displays without side gutters. Touch cards also stay clear of the Backpack control.

The exterior office fits within 360 by 280 logical pixels, down from 500 by 390. The interior artwork uses 60% of its former size while characters retain their normal world size. `Home.officeView` maps the conductor, spawn, exit, and collision floor into the same smaller room. `Home.officeFloor` is a foot-contact polygon authored in the artwork's 960 by 720 coordinates, with a 28-world-pixel offset from actor center to feet. It covers the rear floor up to the cabinets, both sides of the room, and the front threshold while excluding the desk, stove, crates, and walls. Host movement, guest prediction, and save-position clamping share this mask. Valid positions on the upper floor survive reloading.

`game/home_stop.lua` owns the persisted stages, unread dialogue line, office occupancy, supply receipt, repair receipt, floors, and interaction points. Both room transitions are host-authoritative party transactions, with distinct spawn/clamp rules and conductor visibility. New campaigns start with empty train supplies; loading grants 10 each of food, water, coal, and oil exactly once. Initial servicing restores 100% condition without spending journey oil. The host checkpoints each story/preparation action and departure. Clients cannot advance it. Existing campaigns have an empty inactive home state and continue normally.
