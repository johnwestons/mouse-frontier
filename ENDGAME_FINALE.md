# Endgame and Finale

Stop 50 ends the family trail with a saved, positive-only decision instead of a fixed paragraph. `game/finale_progression.lua` is the shared Windows and Android authority for legacy scoring, reunion text, final choices, and the campaign report.

## Legacy score

The finale recognizes the whole journey:

- goodwill and the number of completed help activities;
- all ten family clues and five mystery clues;
- completed passenger rides;
- train condition and acquired cars;
- player level.

The score produces one of three positive legacies: **Home at Last**, **Railway of Hope**, or **Frontier Beacon**. There is no bad, evil, or punishment ending. A lightly completed campaign still reaches family and safety; stronger community ties broaden the good the player leaves behind.

## The Last Switch

Before the campaign report, the player chooses what happens next:

1. **Build a Safe Haven** — turn the valley depot into a home for travelers and separated families.
2. **Rest With Your Family** — let the engine cool and rebuild together in the valley.
3. **Keep the Train Running** — bring the family aboard and carry relief back along the route.

The choice changes the closing legacy text and is stored in the save. Mouse, keyboard number keys, touch, and Android pointer translation use the same three controls. After choosing, the report shows goodwill, clues, help, rides, train condition, cars, and level before returning to the save files.

Save version 27 adds the finale record. Older journeys migrate sequentially and can make the decision when their stop-50 save is opened.
