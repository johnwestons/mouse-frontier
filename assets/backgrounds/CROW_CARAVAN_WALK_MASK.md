# Editable crow caravan walk mask

`walkmask-crow-caravan-campsite-v1.png` belongs to `crow-caravan-campsite-v1.png` in this folder. Both are **1448 × 1086 pixels**.

The mask starts completely white. Leave **white** where characters may walk and paint **black** over blocked ground. Save an opaque PNG with the original filename and dimensions, then restart the desktop game. Mobile needs a rebuilt installation; the packager preserves mask pixels without resizing them.

The mask replaces the old floor and obstacle shapes, with a small clearance around the player's feet and the screen edges. The tents, stalls, wagon, and fire are separate sprites placed over the background, so include their footprints when painting blocked areas. Until painted, the blank mask allows walking across them.
