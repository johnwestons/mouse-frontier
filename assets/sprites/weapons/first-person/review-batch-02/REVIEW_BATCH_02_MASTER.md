## Shared generation brief

- Built-in image generation with the existing inventory sprites as identity and finish references.
- Project-compatible pixel art grounded in the named real-world platform or coherent mechanical donor.
- Hip view: complete rear three-quarter low-ready weapon, no hands or arms, with muzzle and stock/grip fully inside the canvas.
- ADS view: true straight shooter-eye perspective with bore, receiver, stock/grip, rear sight, and front sight on the same centerline.
- Open sights: front post visibly nested inside the rear notch with equal side clearance and level sight tops; aperture sights centered concentrically.
- ADS has exactly zero yaw and zero roll. Detachable magazines and pistol grips remain beneath the receiver centerline and are seen edge-on with strong foreshortening; their broad side profiles never project left or right.
- No reticle, optic, hands, arms, muzzle flash, smoke, text, logo, watermark, extra weapon, or detached parts.

## Current QA status

- Inventory reconciliation: 39 of 39 firearms have both required views when Batch 01 and Batch 02 are combined.
- All selected silhouettes are complete and uncropped.
- The selected ADS corrections use one centered front element inside one rear notch/aperture; rejected separated or duplicate sight drafts remain superseded history only.
- The views preserve the intended platform identities and model-specific sight families. Bead-only shotguns receive no invented rear sight; the Remington 870 base sprite instead keeps its real adjustable rear and ramped front slug/deer sight pair.
- Many built-in outputs contain a baked pale checkerboard in RGB pixels despite transparent-alpha prompting. They are therefore **visual-review candidates, not runtime-ready assets**.
- Some files from the improvised wave received an earlier deterministic alpha cleanup before that workflow was stopped. Raw built-in originals remain preserved in the generator output history. No further scripted image cleanup is authorized or planned.
- No runtime weapon mapping, UI, animation, sound, or gameplay code was changed.

## Production promotion status — 2026-08-30
